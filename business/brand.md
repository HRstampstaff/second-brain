# Brand: Stamp Staff

## Logo

The Stamp Staff logo is a postage-stamp badge: interlocking "SS" mark, a lighthouse, a gator in
an explorer's pith helmet, and "EST. 2017" — nodding to the Islamorada, FL home base. Use it on
client- and VA-facing documents (proposals, agreements, welcome packets, letterhead).

File: `business/logo.png` (add this once we have the image file on disk — see log.md).

## Color palette

Pulled from the logo. Use these for documents, slides, and anything branded.

| Role | Color | Approx. hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / accent | Gold / mustard yellow | `#D4A72C` |
| Text / line art / borders | Black | `#1A1A1A` |
| Background | Cream / warm off-white | `#E8E4D0` |
| Highlight accents | White | `#FFFFFF` |

Pairing: black text/line art on the gold or cream background reads cleanest; use white sparingly
for accents (stripe details, badge text) rather than large fields.

## Usage notes

- Warm but professional tone (see `CLAUDE.md`) — the palette should read the same way: warm
  (gold/cream) but grounded (black), not garish.
- Keep the lighthouse/gator artwork intact when reusing the logo; don't recolor the line art.
