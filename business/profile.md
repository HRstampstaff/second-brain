## Who they are

Stamp Staff, founded 2017, based in Islamorada, FL. Owned and run by Mark and Kristin Stampini together. Mark and Kristin had been hiring Filipino virtual assistants since 2009 to help grow their own real estate business. In 2017 they saw a chance to help other realtors free up their time affordably, while also giving other Filipino VAs free training and long-term work from home. They started with a couple of VAs for Kristin's coaching members; by 2026 Stamp Staff has grown to over 100 virtual assistants. Their stated mission is to bridge the gap between unemployment in the Philippines and helping business owners grow. The team includes 3 coaches who help mentor the VAs (for example, "Coach Ann" is named in a billing email as involved in client conversations). Ailynn Perez handles billing, signing as "Stamp Staff Billing."

## Scope of the business

Stamp Staff is a virtual assistant staffing agency: it recruits, trains, vets, and manages remote Filipino VAs for small and growing US business clients, now over 100 VAs strong. Areas of support include executive and administrative assistance, database and CRM management, lead management and appointment setting, customer service and client communication, marketing and social media support, graphic design and video editing, operations and project coordination, general business support, and calendar and email management. Both part-time and full-time placements are available. VAs work across multiple time zones and adjust to client schedules. Stamp Staff currently has 90 active clients.

## How they operate

Process: free discovery call, then Stamp Staff presents the top 3 pre-vetted candidates, the client interviews and picks one, a Service Agreement is signed by e-signature, an onboarding call is scheduled (with the assigned VA and client), and then ongoing management begins with daily check-ins, performance reports, a dedicated Client Success Manager, and coach mentoring for the VA.

Pricing is a monthly subscription on a 6-month minimum contract: $1,197/month for part-time and $1,997/month for full-time, with a replacement guarantee if a VA is a poor fit.

## Voice and send permission

Tone: warm but professional. The AI team may NOT send anything to a client or VA without Mark or Kristin reading it first.

## Standing rules clients ask about

- 6-month minimum contract
- Replacement guarantee if a VA isn't a good fit
- Monthly service fee can increase over time; increases are communicated in advance (for example, a past increase of $0.50/hour)
- Late fee of $100 applies if payment isn't received by the 3rd of the month
- Clients are asked to prepare onboarding info before the VA starts: main point of contact, preferred communication tool (Google Chat, Slack, Skype, FB Messenger, etc.), logins (CRM, social media), file storage (Dropbox or Google Drive), a work email, company details, and templates/checklists

## Tools and where documents live

- Service Agreements are sent for e-signature via sendlink.co
- Onboarding calls are held over Zoom
- Client templates and checklists are gathered into one Dropbox or Google Drive folder
- Client communication tools vary by client (Google Chat, Slack, Skype, FB Messenger, etc.)
- Client platform logins (CRM, GHL, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube) are shared with the assigned VA as needed

## What the business stands for

In their own words, what they want a client to be able to say: "Stamp Staff gave me a reliable, well-trained virtual professional who truly understands my business, takes ownership of their work, and gives me back the time to focus on growth."

## Who they are best for, and who they are not

Good fit: "established, growing business owners, typically generating at least $250,000 in annual revenue, who are overwhelmed with day-to-day tasks, ready to delegate, and willing to invest time in onboarding, communication, and building a long-term relationship with a remote team member."

Not a good fit: "businesses looking for the cheapest short-term freelancer, expecting immediate results without providing training or direction, unwilling to communicate consistently, or not yet financially stable enough to commit to ongoing staffing support."

## How they sound, in their own words

Tone: "warm but professional." On specific words or phrases: "none. just normal english business and casual language." No special phrases always used, none to avoid.

## What makes them different

From their own About Us page: "Unlike traditional staffing companies, we provide support beyond recruitment. We carefully screen candidates, coordinate interviews, assist with onboarding, and continue to support both the client and the virtual assistant after placement." They also frame their work as a two-sided mission, helping US business owners grow while giving Filipino VAs free training and long-term work from home.
