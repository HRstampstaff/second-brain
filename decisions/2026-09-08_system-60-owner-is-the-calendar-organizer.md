# The System 60 owner is whoever the calendar event is transferred to

**Decided by Ailynn, 2026-09-08.**

Each week's System 60 process-mapping session belongs to whoever owns the process being mapped.
**Ailynn signals that by transferring the Friday calendar event to them, and she re-transfers it
every week.**

So: **the organizer of that week's instance IS the owner.** Not the creator, which stays Kristin
forever, and not the attendee list.

## Why it is written down

Google transfers ownership for the **whole series**, not one occurrence. When 11 Sep went to Ann, so
did 18 Sep, 25 Sep, 2 Oct and 9 Oct, and there is no way to change the owner of a single date.

That means **the organizer field only stays correct because Ailynn keeps re-transferring it.** She
confirmed she will. It is a manual step every week, and the week she forgets, the field will say last
week's owner with complete confidence.

## What follows

- The reminder routine drafts to the organizer, and does not second-guess it.
- If the topic in the title has moved on while the organizer has not changed, it **still drafts to the
  organizer** and says in one line that the transfer may not have been redone. Ailynn decides, not Vera.
- The routine records the organizer it used each week, so there is something to compare against.

Routine: row 3 of the Routines table. Template: `templates/system-60-reminder.md`.
