- [Topic — one line each, plain words. Three to five of them.]

**What we decided**

- [Decision, and who it affects. If nothing was decided, say "Nothing settled yet" rather than
  padding this section.]

**What changes for you**

- [Name]: [the one thing they now do differently, and by when]

**Still open**

- [Item] — [owner] — [by when]

**Next AJ call:** [day and time, or "not booked yet"]

Anything I have missed or written down wrong, reply here and I will correct it before it goes into
the SOP.

Thanks,
Ailynn

## Rules for writing it

- **Three to five bullets per section.** If it is longer than one screen, it will not be read.
- **Every "still open" item has a name and a date against it.** An open item with no owner is not an
  open item, it is a wish.
- **Never write a decision AJ did not actually make.** If the call ended without a decision, the
  email says so. That is the whole value of sending it.
- **Warm but professional**, the same as everything else that goes out under Stamp Staff's name.
