# Ad scripts — Video editor placement

**Draft, 2026-09-07.** Written from Jeremy Haynes' rules on the Sept 7 call. See
`notes/2026-09-07_jeremy-haynes-ad-scripts-verbatim.md`.

Jeremy flagged this as a real opportunity, second to CSM: "Video editing is super in demand...
surprisingly, it's not a position that most people fill in house," and "I don't know anybody who
places video editors." He also confirmed the price point works — he hired a Filipino editor himself
at $1,800/month for effectively unlimited output.

---

## Video ad 1 — the bottleneck

> You need more videos out than you're getting out.
>
> Not because you're short on ideas. Because you're short on an editor.
>
> Ours edit all day, every day. Jump cuts and captions turned around fast enough that filming becomes the only thing you have to do.
>
> We find, train, place and manage full-time video editors inside your business.
>
> Press the button below.

~25 seconds.

## Check before these run

**"turned around fast"** and any volume claim — do not put a number in the ad (a per-day or per-week
count) until we've confirmed what our editors actually deliver. Jeremy's "30 ads in a day" was his
own agency's capability, not ours. Use our real number or no number.
