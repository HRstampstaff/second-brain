This is between the Customer and **Global Virtual Real Estate Assistant LLC, DBA Stamp Staff**.

## 1. Service Packages & Fees

Full-time VA: **$1,997/month** (6-month minimum)

## 2. Key Terms

- **Cancellation:** 30-day written notice required after the minimum commitment. No cancellation
  during the replacement period.
- **Payment:** Due the 1st of each month. A $100 late fee applies if unpaid by the 2nd. Service is
  suspended by the 3rd and canceled by the 7th. (See `policies/billing-cycle.md` for the full policy,
  including the autopay and invoice-without-card tracks.)
- **Autopay required:** Charged every 28th. Primary and backup payment methods required; backup is
  only charged if the primary fails.
- **Benefits:** Customer pays for 5 days PTO (accrued after 12 months) and 4 paid holidays (New
  Year's, Birthday, Thanksgiving, Christmas). Optional annual raise (100% to the VA).

## 3. Non-Hiring Clause

Customer agrees not to hire Stamp Staff employees during employment and for 2 years after
termination. Violation incurs a $15,000 fee plus attorney fees. A buyout option is available for
$15,000.

## 4. Liability, Disclaimers & Confidentiality

- **Content responsibility:** Customer is solely responsible for all content, postings, and images
  used by VAs. Stamp Staff is not liable for misuse.
- **Advertising:** Customer is responsible for all paid advertising expenses, monitoring, and budget
  controls.
- **Website compliance:** Customer is solely responsible for all website compliance. Stamp Staff and
  its VAs bear no responsibility.
- **Payment security:** Customer is strictly prohibited from sharing credit card or payment
  information with VAs. Any sharing of payment credentials is the Customer's sole responsibility.
  Stamp Staff has no liability for misuse, unauthorized transactions, or financial losses resulting
  from the Customer sharing payment information with VAs.
- **Confidentiality:** Stamp Staff keeps all proprietary Customer information, trade secrets, and
  internal data strictly confidential, and ensures VAs adhere to the same standard.
- **Governing law:** Florida. This Agreement replaces all prior agreements.
