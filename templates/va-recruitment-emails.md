# VA recruitment emails

**Version: 1.0 - 2026-09-04**

Source: "VA Recruitment Process Flow" in Google Drive, main draft (see
`procedures/recruitment.md` and `procedures/recruitment-assistant-role.md` for the process these
fit into). Key sends every one of these except the first, which auto-sends from BambooHR on
submission. Fill in the placeholders — never invent wording or promise a rate, date, or link that
isn't confirmed.

---

## 1. Application acknowledgment (auto-sent on submission)

Subject: We've Received Your Application — Stamp Staff

Hi {{APPLICANT_NAME}},

Thank you for applying to Stamp Staff for the {{POSITION}} role. We've received your application
and our recruitment team is currently reviewing it.

If your background matches what we're looking for, you'll hear from us with next steps within a
few business days. We appreciate your interest in joining our team.

Best regards,
Stamp Staff Recruitment Team
