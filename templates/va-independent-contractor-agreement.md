# Independent Contractor's Agreement (VA)

**Version: 2026 - 2026-09-03**

The current VA-side contract, confirmed by Ailynn 2026-09-03 (the "2026 VA Contract" version
supersedes an older draft that said 3-week training; this one is signed and live). Transcribed from a
real signed copy for reference; personal details of the signing VA are deliberately left out of this
file — only the general terms below apply to every VA. Owen fills in the VA's name and address when a
new hire signs; nobody invents wording beyond that.

Between **StampStaff Remote Teams and Talent** and the Independent Contractor (VA).

## Salary

- **$10/day for a 4-week training period** — minimum 8 hours per training day.
- **$4/hour post-training.**
- First pay lands once onboarded with a client and the VA has worked/accumulated at least 3 days
  during the cutoff period nearest to onboarding. First payroll = training allowance + worked-days
  compensation. If fewer than 3 days worked in the initial cutoff, those days roll into the next
  cutoff along with the training fee.
- Performance appraisal on the 12-month anniversary with the client; increase amount is
  client-dependent. **Pay always resets to the $4/hour base if the VA is removed or replaced by the
  client.**
- After 1 year of continuous service with the same client: 5 leave credits annually (see
  [policies/va-paid-time-off.md](../policies/va-paid-time-off.md) for accrual detail). Leave credit
  eligibility resets if the VA loses the client — must complete a new full year with the new client
  before earning leave credits again.
- **Pay dates: the 5th and 20th of the month** (see
  [policies/payroll-cutoff.md](../policies/payroll-cutoff.md)).

## Paid Holidays

US or Canadian Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, New Year's Day, and the VA's Birthday — VA must have
already passed training and been endorsed to a client for at least 30 days to qualify. If a paid
holiday falls on the VA's day off, an additional paid day off is earned instead.

## HMO Coverage

- After 12 months of service, full-time status qualifies a VA for HMO coverage plus 1 dependent.
  Requires complete documentation submitted to enroll.
- **Maintaining it:** must stay full-time (40+ hrs/week). Dropping below full-time gives a 30-day
  grace period to return to 40+ hours before HMO cards (VA and dependent) are canceled. Re-enrollment
  is available at the July renewal period or anytime after resuming full-time status.

## Independent contractor status

VA operates as an independent business, sets their own manner/method of work, and is free from the
Company's direction except as to results. Provides their own hardware, software, headset, internet,
and workspace. Responsible for their own taxes and filings; invoices the Company for fees. Cannot work
for, commit to, or bind any other company while working with Stamp Staff without authorization.

## Restrictions

- May NOT receive money or anything of value from a Client directly or indirectly without Company
  consent.
- May NOT work for a Client, or work additional hours outside contract hours, without prior Company
  consent. Daily extensions need advance written approval routed through the coach; permanent
  additional hours need advance client notice to the company/coach.
- May NOT solicit or accept business ventures with any Client, employee, contractor, agent, or
  consultant of the Company without consent.
- May NOT engage in business/employment similar to the Company's without consent, or solicit
  Company staff/clients to leave.
- **Breach of any term: $2,000 liquidated damages**, deductible from final fees, without prejudice to
  further civil/criminal action.

## To get paid

- Clock in/out using company-provided tools (proof of attendance) — see
  [policies/va-clock-in-out-rules.md](../policies/va-clock-in-out-rules.md).
- Send SOD (Start of Day) and EOD (End of Day) reports — see
  [policies/va-code-of-conduct.md](../policies/va-code-of-conduct.md).
- Respond to clients/superiors within 10 minutes during shifts.
- Submit monthly KPIs.

## Termination

- **Involuntary:** immediate for breach — inducing staff/clients to leave, disloyalty, fraud, false
  statements, policy violations, disclosing confidential info, failing to return company property.
  Exit form + Quit Claim form (digital signature) required; final pay released the 5th or 20th after
  payroll receives the signed Quit Claim, not exceeding 30 days.
- **Voluntary/resignation:** 30 days' notice if with an active client, in writing to the coach (cc
  HR). Must help train the replacement and hand over materials/logins by the last day. Same
  clearance/Quit Claim/final-pay process as above.
- **Immediate resignation** (not rendering the 30 days): final pay is forfeited, except when the VA
  has no active client (still must complete clearance).

## Non-compete / confidentiality

18 months post-termination: no disclosure or use of Confidential Information; no ownership,
management, employment, or consultancy with a competing business; no soliciting Company staff,
contractors, or clients for employment/freelance work elsewhere. Poaching a Company contact costs the
Company's training/educational expenses or $2,000 liquidated damages if none were spent. A client
transfer through the VA's solicitation requires reimbursing the Company's expenses and lost profit for
18 months.

This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, employment, franchise, or agency
relationship.
