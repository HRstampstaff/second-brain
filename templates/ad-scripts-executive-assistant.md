# Ad scripts — Executive Assistant placement

**Draft, 2026-09-07.** Written from Jeremy Haynes' rules on the Sept 7 call. His verbatim EA script
is in `notes/2026-09-07_jeremy-haynes-ad-scripts-verbatim.md`.

Jeremy's warning on this one: EAs have demand but "that one I think requires a little bit of an
education gap," and the VA/EA space is "probably slightly more saturated" than CSM. Run CSM first.
These are for when CSM is dialed in.

His angle: go after the pain, not the role. The pile of small jobs nobody is doing.

---

## Video ad 1 — the pile

> There's a pile of small things in your business that nobody is doing.
>
> Every one of them is below somebody's pay grade, so it gets pushed. Easy to write off — until the pile is the problem.
>
> That's an executive assistant.
>
> We find one, train them, place them, and manage them inside your business. The pile disappears.
>
> Press the button below.

~25 seconds.

## Video ad 2 — shorter

> All the little things that aren't getting done aren't getting done because they're below everybody's pay grade.
>
> Until there are enough of them that it's a real problem.
>
> A trained, managed executive assistant fixes that permanently.
>
> We find them, train them, and manage them for you. Press the button below.

~20 seconds.
