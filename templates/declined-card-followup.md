**Version: 1.0 - 2026-09-02**

Use when a client's credit card on file was declined for their monthly subscription, an initial
notice already went out, and there has been no reply. This is a second-touch follow-up, not the
first notice. Fill in the placeholders from Fiona (amount, due date, card on file, late fee terms).

Fiona gives every figure in this template. Never fill a placeholder with a guessed number.
This is an ordinary payment reminder (Tessa's to draft). If a client disputes the charge or raises
a legal question, that goes to Vera, not this template.
