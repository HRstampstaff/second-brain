# Unpaid invoice follow-up (no card on file)

**Version: 1.0 - 2026-09-02**

Use for clients who pay by invoice (QuickBooks) rather than autopay, when the monthly invoice has
gone unpaid past the due date. This is the first/standard notice, not a repeat follow-up. Fill in
the placeholders from Fiona (invoice number, date, amount, due date) and QuickBooks.

Fiona gives every figure in this template. Never fill a placeholder with a guessed number.
This is an ordinary payment reminder (Tessa's to draft). If a client disputes the invoice or raises
a legal question, that goes to Vera, not this template.
