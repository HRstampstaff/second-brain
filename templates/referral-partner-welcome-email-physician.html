What Happens After the Introduction
1
We will contact the referral and schedule an inquiry call with Kristin’s husband, Mark.
2
During the call, Mark will learn more about the individual, their business, their personal responsibilities, and where they need the most support.
3
We will explore how a virtual executive assistant could help free up their time. This may include personal support such as:
•  Coordinating family appointments
•  Managing calendars
•  Handling travel arrangements
•  Organizing personal tasks
•  Assisting with other responsibilities outside of their medical practice
We can also support their business or office operations when needed.
4
If they decide to move forward, we will present qualified virtual assistant candidates and schedule interviews so they can select the person who is the best fit.
5
Once they choose their virtual assistant, we will guide them through the onboarding process. They will also be assigned a mentor coach who will support both the client and the virtual assistant.

We train the virtual assistant during the first month and provide weekly mentoring during the initial onboarding period. Their coach can also help the virtual assistant implement systems, project management tools, workflows, and other processes in the client’s personal life or business.

If a virtual assistant ever needs to be replaced, we provide replacement support at no additional placement cost for the life of the client relationship.
