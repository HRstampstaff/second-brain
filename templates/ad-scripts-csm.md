# Ad scripts — CSM placement

**Draft, 2026-09-07.** Written from Jeremy Haynes' rules on the Sept 7 call. His verbatim wording is
in `notes/2026-09-07_jeremy-haynes-ad-scripts-verbatim.md` — that file is the source, this one is the
copy to actually film.

CSM leads because Jeremy said it's the open lane: "The CSM advertising opportunity is huge... nobody
else is doing it and there's nobody else there."

## The rules these follow

1. Say the outcome in as few words as possible. If it takes long to say, it's wrong.
2. Never say "business owner." Say their problem, their circumstance, or their outcome instead.
3. Assume they know what a CSM is. Explaining it attracts people who aren't in market.
4. Get direct immediately after the hook. No wind-up.
5. One clear button call to action at the end.

## Video ad 1 — lead script (keeps the hook Jeremy approved)

> Years ago, closing a new client meant a week of onboarding chaos. Emails, the logins, all the handholding, all of it. Now I close a client and go do this instead.
>
> A CSM took it over.
>
> Ours onboard your new clients, activate them fast, and make sure they actually get a result. Your churn drops. Your customers stay.
>
> We find, place, train and manage top talent CSMs inside your business.
>
> If you're looking for your first or next CSM, press the button below.

~30 seconds. The first two lines are the hook Jeremy said worked — everything after is the part he
called mumbo jumbo, rewritten direct.

## Video ad 2 — the failed search

> You've been looking for a great CSM and you can't find one.
>
> Your recruiting isn't working. The seat is still empty. And every new client you close is landing on a team that's already full.
>
> A CSM fixes that. Lower churn, faster activation, and honest feedback from the people actually using what you sell.
>
> We find them, train them, place them, and manage them on an ongoing basis.
>
> Top talent CSM placement. Press the button below.

~30 seconds.

## Video ad 3 — churn, outcome first

> Most people hire a CSM for one reason. Churn.
>
> New client comes in. The CSM onboards them, activates them, makes sure they get what they paid for — and they stay.
>
> We find, train, place and manage top talent CSMs inside your business.
>
> If you need one, press the button below.

~20 seconds. Shortest of the three — worth testing against the longer two.

## Video ad 4 — whole department

> If you need three CSMs, or five, or ten, placed immediately, we'll build you the department.
>
> Customer success or customer service. We source them, train them, manage them, and replace them if one doesn't work out.
>
> If the volume of deals you're closing has outgrown the people handling them, press the button below.

~20 seconds. This is the one for bigger companies where the owner isn't the decision maker.

## Check before these run

Three things in here are claims, not copy. Confirm each is true of what Stamp Staff actually delivers
before any of it goes live:

1. **"replace them if one doesn't work out"** (video 4) — confirm the replacement terms match the
   Service Agreement.
2. **"train them"** — appears in every script. Confirm what training we actually do, so the sales
   call can back it up.
3. **"fully managed"** vs placement only — the page headline promises managed. Make sure the ad and
   the page point at the same offer, or the drop-off Jeremy flagged gets worse, not better.
