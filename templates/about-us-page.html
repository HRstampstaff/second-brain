Why we started

It worked for our own business first

We are Mark and Kristin Stampini, and we had been hiring Filipino virtual assistants since 2009 to help grow our own real estate business. It worked. By 2017 we could see the same thing working for the realtors around us, who were doing far too much of their own admin and had no affordable way to stop.

So we started Stamp Staff with a couple of virtual assistants supporting Kristin's coaching members. Since then we have placed more than 2,000 virtual assistants and helped over 1,000 businesses across the United States get their time back.

Our mission

It runs in both directions

We exist to bridge the gap between unemployment in the Philippines and business owners who need to grow. Those are the same problem seen from two sides.

Our clients get back the hours they were spending on work somebody else could do better. Our virtual assistants get free training, a career they can build on, and long-term work from home, which in the Philippines is rare and worth protecting.

We take both halves seriously. It is why we screen as hard as we do, and why we keep coaching people long after they are placed.

How it works

Five steps from first call to first week

  1. 1

    A discovery call, free

    We learn what you do, where your time actually goes, and what you would hand over first if you could hand over anything.

  2. 2

    We present the top three candidates

    Pre-vetted, and screened against the work you described rather than pulled off a résumé pile.

  3. 3

    You interview, and you choose

    You pick the person you want to work with. We do not assign anybody to you.

  4. 4

    Agreement and onboarding

    The Service Agreement is signed by e-signature, then we run an onboarding call with you and your new assistant together so the first week is not guesswork.

  5. 5

    Then the part most agencies skip

    Daily check-ins, performance reporting, and a mentor coach assigned to your assistant for as long as they work with you.

What they do

The work our assistants take off your desk

  • Executive and administrative assistance
  • Calendar and email management
  • Database and CRM management
  • Lead management and appointment setting
  • Customer service and client communication
  • Marketing and social media support

Part-time and full-time placements are both available. Our assistants work across US time zones and adjust to your schedule rather than the other way around.

After placement

Recruiting is where most agencies stop

Unlike traditional staffing companies, we provide support beyond recruitment. We carefully screen candidates, coordinate interviews, assist with onboarding, and continue to support both the client and the virtual assistant after placement.

Stamp Staff

In practice that means three mentor coaches supporting our assistants, daily check-ins through onboarding, and performance reporting you actually receive.

It also means a replacement guarantee. If the person we placed turns out not to be the right fit, we replace them at no additional placement cost, for the life of your relationship with us.

Fit

We would rather be honest about this up front

Where we do our best work

  • Established, growing business owners, typically at $250,000 or more in annual revenue
  • Owners who are overwhelmed by day-to-day work and genuinely ready to delegate it
  • People willing to invest real time in onboarding and communication
  • Anyone who wants a long-term relationship with a remote team member, not a transaction

Where we are probably not the right fit

  • Businesses looking for the cheapest short-term freelancer
  • Owners expecting immediate results without providing training or direction
  • Anyone unwilling to communicate consistently
  • Businesses not yet stable enough to commit to ongoing staffing support. We ask for a six-month minimum
