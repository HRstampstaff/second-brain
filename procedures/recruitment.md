# Recruiting a VA

follows the more complete, two-round version. Owen should re-check against the live Drive doc before

## When recruitment gets triggered

1. Every Wednesday, before/during the weekly meeting, check the VA Available tracker.
2. Trigger if: EA count is below 12, or Creative/SMM count is below 8.
3. Trigger immediately, don't wait for Wednesday, if: a client cancels, a VA resigns, or a client has
   a specialized need not currently covered.
4. Regular training batches run the 2nd Monday of each month. An off-cycle batch only happens for a
   specialized immediate need or a below-threshold headcount.

## Step 1: Request comes in

Coach/Sales posts in the group chat: client name, role (EA / Creative-SMM / other), reason for the
request, specific skills, shift/timezone, timeline, and internal-or-external sourcing preference.
Ann acknowledges within 24 hours.

## Step 2: Check for an internal match

Janet checks the VA Available tracker / resume bank. If there's a match: Mock call with Mark and the
coach, then if that passes, Janet sets up the client interview. Logged as "Filled Internally" or moves
to external sourcing.

## Step 3: Job description and ad

Ann pulls the standard must-haves doc (see Recruitment Non-Negotiables below), Kate drafts the JD
with any client-specific additions, Ann sends it for sign-off. New role gets new ad copy; an existing
standard role reuses the existing ad.

## Step 4: Post the ad

Ann/Key post to the relevant Facebook groups and the internal referral group chat, and share to
coaches. Organic only, no paid boosting.

## Step 5: Applicants come in

Applicants apply via BambooHR (contact info, resume, DISC, speedtest, speaking test, WhatsApp number).
Key logs every applicant into the Recruitment Tracker, qualified or not.

## Step 6: Screening and initial interview

Key screens against must-haves, updates status to Screened-Qualified or Screened-Unqualified. Qualified
applicants get a WhatsApp call to schedule the initial interview. Initial interviewer: EA → Janet or
Ann; SMM → Marmil or Ben; client-specific → the hiring coach.

## Step 7: Initial interview

Checklist scorecard: experience/work history, availability & equipment, client-specific, communication
concern (assessed last). For EA, an obvious communication concern auto-fails. For SMM it's a flag only,
carried forward as a note. Pass moves to Assessment; fail goes to the regret/redirect email.

## Step 8: Assessment

Key sends the role-specific assessment link (EA: English/typing + admin practical task; SMM: video
edit + thumbnail + portfolio). Applicant has 24 hours.

## Step 9: Final interview scheduling

Star-rating scorecard interviewer: EA → Kate; SMM → Raf. Reviews the submitted assessment before or at
the start of the call.

## Step 10: Final interview

Categories: skills & technical ability, English communication, culture fit, assessment/practical task
review, client-specific. If passed, the interviewer tells the applicant on the spot: **$4/hr once
onboarded, $10/day during the 4-week training period, flat rate, no negotiation**, plus the training
date.

## Step 11: Notify the applicant

Within 24-48 hours: regret (failed initial or final), Pass + Training Date (if a date is set), or
Pass + Pooled (no date yet). Pooled candidates get re-screened at the 60-day mark before being
considered active again.

## Step 12: Orientation and training

SMM/Creative batches need a minimum of 5 candidates to start; fewer roll into the next batch once the
current one is 5 days in. Key sets up the batch's Google Chat and Discord, sends the Pre-Training
Checklist right after passing, and the Orientation + Day 1 Details email the Friday before start.

## Recruitment non-negotiables (any position)

- At least college level (units or graduate)
- Minimum 6 months night shift experience within the last 3 months, with US/Canada account experience
- Proficient in MS Office, Google Workspace, and AI tools
- Not currently employed elsewhere once training starts; full commitment during training
- Main PC (personal use only) + backup PC, both within spec; primary + backup internet at 100mbps
  minimum; working webcam; noise-cancelling headset
- Quiet, private work area
- Good verbal and written English
