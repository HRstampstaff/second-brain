## Role overview

Key owns the applicant's journey from the moment they apply until they either start training or
are notified they're not moving forward: scheduling, notifying, tracking, and prepping. Key does
**not** conduct interviews or make pass/fail decisions — that's the interviewers' job (see
recruitment.md's Interviewer Procedure).

## Daily checklist (4-hour workday)

Work through these in order every day so nothing silently stalls in the pipeline:

1. **Check for new applicants.** Open BambooHR, click into each open job posting. For every new
   applicant, add a row to the Recruitment Tracker: date applied, name, phone, email, position,
   status = "New."
2. **Screen new/logged applicants.** Compare against the role's must-haves (education, night-shift
   experience, US/Canada + actual VA experience — not just BPO/call center, equipment). Update
   status to "Screened-Qualified" or "Screened-Unqualified." Unqualified applicants stay logged, no
   further action.
3. **Send Interview Notice (Initial) and schedule via WhatsApp.** For every "Screened-Qualified"
   applicant: send the Interview Notice (Initial) email, call via WhatsApp to offer 2-3 time slots,
   once confirmed send Interview Confirmation (Initial) with date/time/Gmeet link, update status to
   "Initial Interview Scheduled," assign the interviewer (Janet or Ann for EA; Marmil or Ben for
   SMM) and send them a calendar invite with the Gmeet link.
4. **Check for completed Initial Interviews.** "Initial Passed" → send the Assessment Invite email
   with the correct form link (SMM or EA). "Initial Failed" → skip to step 8 (Notify).
5. **Check for overdue assessments.** If 24+ hours pass since the Assessment Invite with no
   submission, update status to "Assessment Overdue," do not follow up, and send the fail notice
   for failure to submit. They can reapply after 3 months.
6. **Review completed assessments.** Check the Google Form's Responses tab (or the notification
   email) to confirm the submission is complete. Update status to "Assessment Completed."
7. **Send Interview Notice (Final) and schedule via WhatsApp.** Same process as step 3, using the
   Final templates. Assign the Final interviewer: Kate for EA, Raf for SMM. Update status to
   "Final Interview Scheduled."
8. **Check for completed Final Interviews.** Send the matching outcome email (Pass + Training Date,
   Pass + Pooled, or Regret/Redirect) within 24-48 hours.
9. **Check pooled candidates.** Filter for status = "Pooled." Anyone pooled 60+ days gets flagged
   for re-screening (see Managing the Pool below).
10. **If a training batch is coming up**, work through Batch Prep below.

## Batch prep

Once recruitment.md's Step 12 (Orientation & Training) confirms a training date:

1. Notify all coaches of the upcoming batch: batch number/date, names of expected trainees, role
   per trainee.
2. Set up the batch's Google Chat (Training Communication Channel) and Discord channel/group
   ahead of the start date; add confirmed trainees once their training date is locked in.
3. Right after passing, send the Pre-Training Checklist email. On the Friday before a Monday
   start (or equivalent lead time for other start days), send the Orientation + Day 1 Details
   email.
4. Update the Recruitment Tracker status to "Orientation/Training Scheduled."

## Managing the pool (60-day re-screen)

1. **Identify what stage they were pooled at:**
   - Tier A — screened only, never interviewed
   - Tier B — completed one interview under the old (pre-restructure) single-interview process
   - Tier C — completed both Initial + Final under the current two-interview process
2. **Quick check-in (all tiers):** message/call via WhatsApp to confirm they're still interested,
   still available, and equipment/internet still meets requirements.
3. **Route by tier:**
   - Tier A → treat as freshly Screened-Qualified, restart at scheduling the Initial Interview
   - Tier B → their old interview counts as satisfying Initial; skip straight to the Assessment
     Invite, then continue into Final Interview scheduling
   - Tier C → no interviews needed, go straight to training batch scheduling once a slot opens
4. **If unresponsive:** 3 contact attempts over 5-7 business days (Day 1, Day 3-4, Day 6-7), then
   mark "Pool Expired." If they respond but say they're no longer interested, mark "Declined"
   instead.
