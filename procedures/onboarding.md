# Onboarding a new client

Source: "Onboarding Checklist" in Google Drive, a reusable checklist filled in per client. Full copy
below; reset it for each new client.

## Part 1: Pre-onboarding (coach)

0. **Service Agreement & payment confirmation** — before the onboarding presentation:
   - Check the Service Agreement is signed in GoHighLevel (GHL): open the client's contact profile,
     Documents tab, confirm "Remote Staff Service Agreement" shows signed.
   - If not signed, remind Ann to follow up with the client.
   - If still not signed, resend from GHL: Documents tab → three-dot menu → View source → Resend,
     then email the link to the client.
   - Confirm payment with Finance (or Revenue, if you have access).
   - Only proceed to the onboarding presentation once both are confirmed.

## Part 2: Client onboarding

1. **Communication setup**: preferred channel (aside from email), response time expectations,
   check-in schedule (daily/weekly), who the VA reports to, VA working hours and days off, VA coach
   added to the group chat for the first 4 weeks. Urgent matters go to the coach directly at
   VA.Coach.GVACO@gmail.com.
2. **Daily reporting & task tracking**: walk through SOD/EOD reports with the VA, share the Daily Task
   Tracker with the client, confirm speed test results are included in SOD/EOD emails.
3. **Team Leader–VA calls**: schedule a weekly 30-minute Zoom (VA, TLVA, Coach) and a monthly
   30-minute Zoom. Cancellation policy: confirm the day before, notify a day ahead to cancel.
4. **30-60-90 day goals**: identify the 3-5 highest-impact tasks for the VA to own first, define what
   "doing a good job" looks like at 30 days, agree on the first-step protocol when something goes
   wrong, confirm how often progress updates happen, share the VA Evaluation Sheet.
5. **What we need from the client**: access credentials for client-specific tools, any required
   software licenses, the list of systems the VA will work in, brand assets (logos, colors, fonts,
   style guide).
6. **Agreements & ongoing support**: review the Non-Solicitation Agreement (no direct hiring of a
   Stamp Staff VA during or for two years after the engagement; breach fee $15,000 + attorney fees;
   VA penalty for accepting direct employment during engagement is $2,000), confirm the client knows
   how to reach VA coaches any time (VA.Coach.GVACO@gmail.com, no need to wait for the next TLVA
   call), confirm the VA training resource site access.
