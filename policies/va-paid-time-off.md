# VA paid time off (PTO)

Set by Ailynn, 2026-09-01.

- **Filing:** PTO must be filed in BambooHR at least two weeks before the planned time off.
- **Earned PTO:** After 1 year with a client, a VA becomes eligible for 5 days of paid time off
  per year with that client. It accrues monthly at 5/12 of a day per month, not all 5 upfront: a
  VA can only take days they have actually accrued so far.
- **Company-paid holidays**, separate from accrued PTO above:
  - The VA's birth month (a day off)
  - Christmas Day (December 25)
  - New Year's Day (January 1)
  - Thanksgiving: October for Canadian clients, November for US clients
- **Sick with PTO available:** the VA asks the client's approval to use their accrued paid time
  off.
- **Unplanned absence:** the VA can make up the missed hours with the client's approval, and must
  let their coach know.
- **Client-granted extra time off or client-paid holiday:** gets filed in BambooHR too.
