# VA payroll cutoff

Set by Ailynn, 2026-09-02.

Two cutoffs a month:

- **26th (previous month) → 10th (current month)**, paid on the **20th**.
- **11th → 25th (current month)**, paid on the **5th of the next month**.

Coaches approve PTO in BambooHR before each cutoff closes (see
[policies/va-paid-time-off.md](va-paid-time-off.md)) so timesheets are accurate before payroll runs.
