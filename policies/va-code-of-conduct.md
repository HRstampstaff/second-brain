# VA Code of Conduct

**Version: 2026 - 2026-09-03**

Confirmed by Ailynn 2026-09-03. Transcribed from the current signed HR packet (BambooHR e-signature);
personal details of the signing VA are deliberately left out — only the general policy applies to
every VA.

## Additional training classes

Management may periodically require extra training classes. VAs get at least 1 week's written notice,
naming the instructor, date, and time.

## Client endorsement

A VA's job is not guaranteed if the client leaves, but if in good standing, Stamp Staff aims to match
and interview the VA with a new client within 1 month or less.

## Client relationship

VAs may not receive pay directly from a client in any form (puts benefits at risk), may not discuss
contract terms with the client, and must immediately inform Stamp Staff of reduced tasks or a
management change at the client.

## Communication

Issues, agreed additional hours, or anything else must go to the Client Services Manager and Team
Leader (TL) in writing, by email.

## Daily reports (SOD / EOD)

- **Start of Day (SOD):** sent at least 5 minutes before the scheduled shift, listing the tasks
  expected to be accomplished. Emailed to coach and client.
- **End of Day (EOD):** sent at the end of the shift, summarizing tasks actually covered. Emailed to
  coach and client.
- Failing to send either report can result in a written offense and/or non-payment for that shift.

## Offense tiers and recourse

Recourse is the same ladder for tiers 1–3: 1st offense = written/initial warning, 2nd = final written
warning, 3rd = $0.25/hr deducted for one pay period, 4th = $0.50/hr for two pay periods, 5th = $1.00/hr
for three pay periods.

**Tier 1 — minor:** not cooperating on projects, abusing break privileges, tardiness (2x/month or 15
min total, or any tardiness in the first 3 months with a client), sleeping on shift, filing vacation
leave within the first 3 months with a client, late/incomplete selection-and-training paperwork or
required PC/internet upgrades, accepting client gifts for leverage.

**Tier 2 — moderate:** slack time, refusing agreed extended hours/holiday work without justifiable
reason, malingering, non-disclosure of a health condition affecting productivity, revealing
confidential info/trade secrets without authority, dishonesty (falsifying records, concealing facts),
deliberate slowdowns, or failing to notify the Company when unable to work (also forfeits that day's
pay).

**Tier 3 — major (suspension or termination):** abandonment of post, unexcused absence in the first 3
months, an invalid absence reason (valid reasons: emergencies/life-threatening situations involving
self or immediate family, domestic violence, force majeure — NOT minor health issues, oversleeping,
personal errands, or commute problems), negligence of duty, poor performance, encouraging a fellow VA
to break policy, unauthorized absence of more than 2 scheduled workdays, insubordination, harassment
(including discriminatory or threatening remarks), refusing a requested medical certificate.
Consequences: suspension/termination, forfeiture of that day's pay, replacement recommendation. **A
second major offense automatically terminates the contract.**

**Tier 4 — principal (possible termination on first offense):** criminal acts proven in court;
disrespecting a client; misrepresenting the client or Stamp Staff; personal use of client resources
(e.g. their Skype credits); tampering with client records.

Questions on policy go to the VA's coach.
